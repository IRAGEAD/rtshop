description='''
A growing roster of fantasy mounts to find, tame, breed and ride, with new ones joining every month. It started with a unicorn my wife and I could ride together, and grew from there.

On the ground: Unicorn, Polar Bear, Doom Horse, War Mammoth, Frost Sabrecat, Crystal Stag, Qilin (it leaps again in mid-air, on clouds), Kitsune, Moonwolf, Mossback Treant (three riders in its branches), Warhorn Rhino (archers in its howdah) and Jackalope.
In the air: Pegasus, Griffin, Phoenix (its wings whip up a flame tornado), Luna Moth (only under the moon), Sky Whale (six riders on its deck), Emerald Dragonfly (the fastest flyer) and Nimbus Ram (it floats on a cloud and makes it rain).
In water: Dragon Turtle and Tide Narwhal. Up walls: Giant Spider.

Very rarely a mount is shiny: each one has a shiny form of its own, with new colours, glowing details, extra parts and a sparkling aura, and it greets you with a chime. Mounts carry one to six riders, each with their own seat, and have abilities such as frost walking, fire breath, rebirth, a war mammoth's archer seat, foxfire and moon dust. Saddles, armour and saddlebags, coat variants and markings, rolled stats and breeding, and a HUD for stamina and abilities.

Mounts grow with you: a tamed mount levels up as you ride it, use its abilities, fight beside it and keep it near you, unlocking new abilities and growing stronger in its own way. Each level brings a skill point for its own skill tree, where branches lead to powerful capstones (some rule each other out, so choose), new abilities, or new ways to move, from climbing walls to gliding. A Renewal Tonic lets its owner reset the tree. Press K (or use the tab on its inventory) to see its stats, abilities and skills.

Mount Whistle - register your mount, then call it to follow you.
Diamond Carrot - tames a unicorn.
Golden Carrot Stew - helps you find unicorns in the wild.
Renewal Tonic - lets you reset your mount's skill tree.

Mounts, spawns, stats, unlock levels and skill trees are data-driven and can be changed with data packs; server owners can cap flight, riders and abilities, and tune or switch off levelling, in the config.
